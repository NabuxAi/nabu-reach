UNOFFICIAL PATH

QR linking

Device-linked access through a QR session, where supported by a separately reviewed connector.

Potential advantages

  • No new business number may be needed
  • Can work alongside the phone app
  • May preserve existing chat context

Important limitations

  • Not the official Meta Cloud API
  • Session stability and policy risks
ELIGIBILITY REQUIRED

Coexistence

Use an eligible WhatsApp Business App number with the official Business Platform.

Potential advantages

  • Eligible businesses may keep their number
  • Official onboarding route
  • App and API use can coexist

Important limitations

  • Eligibility varies by business
  • Some app features may change
RECOMMENDED

WABA / Cloud API

The official WhatsApp Business Platform for approved business communications.

Advantages

  • Official Meta platform and webhooks
  • Approved message templates
  • Compliance and delivery reporting

Requirements & costs

  • Business setup and review may apply
  • Meta messaging fees may apply

NABU REACH / CONNECTION GUIDE

Connection guide

Unofficial · Not enabled in this release

QR-based device sessions are different from Meta's official API. They can disconnect, impose feature restrictions or violate platform terms, depending on the connector.

1. Review the provider's licensing and platform-policy compliance.

2. Assess account privacy, session storage and operational risk.

3. Use the official WABA route for production outreach.

Official route · Eligibility must be verified

Meta's Business App coexistence can allow eligible businesses to use a number with the Business Platform while retaining compatible app usage. Availability, app behavior and onboarding requirements vary.

1. Verify business and phone-number eligibility in Meta's official onboarding flow.

2. Review supported app features, permissions and messaging costs.

3. Complete approved onboarding through the official gateway once enabled.

Meta documentation ↗
Official Meta Cloud API · Recommended for production

This preview can register a Meta Cloud API placeholder, not connect a real account. Production onboarding will use the existing Nabu WhatsApp MCP gateway after authentication, consent checks and security review.

1. Prepare Meta business details and an eligible phone number.

2. Complete Meta's official business onboarding and permissions.

3. Verify consent, approved templates, rates and applicable fees.
